# ALBUM I — CROWN FIRE SEASON
## The Five-Album Collabo Cycle · Led by CROWN FIRE (Fire Triad: IGNITE · BLAZE · TRAVEL)

GHOST-WRITING DRAFTS — the crews' own bars; Qasparr & Qveen Bee
features ghost-drafted, marked; DRAFT until the founder stamps.

Formation law (founder's ruling, 2026-11-10): the BODY of every
song is the crews' own bars, written and carried by the members
themselves in their own voices and seats. QASPARR and QVEEN BEE
FEATURE — his feature verse, her feature hook or verse. Feature
texts are ghost-DRAFTS in their chartered voices, labeled
[GHOST DRAFT for Qasparr] / [GHOST DRAFT for Qveen Bee]. When
the founder writes his own feature bars himself, they replace
the ghost draft byte for byte.

Voices: Qasparr — the 2Pac register: medium pitch, forward and
urgent, crystal diction, emotion at the front, Detroit /
Brightmoor ground. Qveen Bee — Eve's flow + Da Brat's
linguistics for rap verses; hooks SUNG, alto to soprano,
Lauryn Hill soul power. Sol Rey (Leo) — the Captain, anthem
voice. Ari Sparkz (Aries) — percussive double-time, verses
that start mid-explosion. Sage Archer (Sagittarius) — sung
hooks with a bow's range, the voice that travels.

Detroit bounce throughout. Themes: beginnings, ignition, the
season turning, Brightmoor / Detroit pride, the crown earned —
never given.
