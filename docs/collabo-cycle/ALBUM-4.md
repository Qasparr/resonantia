# ALBUM IV — THE EARTH UNDER US

GHOST-WRITING DRAFTS — the crews' own bars; Qasparr & Qveen Bee
features ghost-drafted, marked; DRAFT until the founder stamps.

The unseated triad's album. Earth took no crew seat in the
engine — Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn stayed with the House as its
ground — so this record belongs to all three crews together,
built on the house ground: BUILD · KEEP · ENDURE. Fire brings
the will, Water brings the memory, Air brings the word; the
Earth under all of it brings the bill, the deed, and the grave
that remembers every name. Formation law stands on every
track: the BODY is the crews' own bars in their own voices;
QASPARR and QVEEN BEE feature — his verse, her hook or verse —
each feature marked GHOST DRAFT, awaiting the founder's stamp
or his own hand, which replaces any ghost byte for byte.
