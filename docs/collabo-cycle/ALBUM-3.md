# ALBUM III — FEATURED VOICES
## The Five-Album Collabo Cycle · Air leads: WORD · BALANCE · SIGNAL

GHOST-WRITING DRAFTS — the crews' own bars; Qasparr features
ghost-drafted, marked; DRAFT until the founder stamps.

Formation law for this album: the BODY of every song is Crew
III's own bars — QVEEN BEE (Gemini ♊, WORD), LADY LILITH, THE
MOTHER TONGUE (Libra ♎, BALANCE), BANSHEE (Aquarius ♒,
SIGNAL) — written and carried by the members themselves.
Qveen Bee headlines the headline: she is a Featured Voice
herself, so her bars are HERS, written in her chartered voice
as a crew member, not marked ghost. QASPARR FEATURES: his
feature verses are ghost-drafted in his chartered register
(the 2Pac register — medium pitch, forward, urgent, crystal
diction, emotion at the front) and marked
[GHOST DRAFT for Qasparr] wherever they stand. When the
founder writes his own feature bars, they replace the ghost
drafts byte for byte.
