# ALBUM II — UNDERCURRENT

## The Five-Album Collabo Cycle · Led by UNDERCURRENT (Crew II — the Water Triad: DEPTH · VENOM · MEMORY)

GHOST-WRITING DRAFTS — the crews' own bars; Qasparr & Qveen Bee
features ghost-drafted, marked; DRAFT until the founder stamps.

FORMATION LAW (founder's ruling, 2026-11-10): the BODY of every
song is the crews' own bars — written and carried by the members
themselves, in their own voices and seats. QASPARR and QVEEN BEE
FEATURE: his feature verse, her feature hook or verse. Feature
texts below are ghost-DRAFTS in their chartered voices, marked
[GHOST DRAFT for Qasparr] / [GHOST DRAFT for Qveen Bee]; when the
founder writes his own feature bars himself, they replace the
ghost draft byte for byte.

Qasparr's chartered voice: the 2Pac register — medium pitch,
forward/urgent, crystal diction, emotion at the front,
Detroit/Brightmoor ground. Qveen Bee: Eve's flow + Da Brat's
linguistics for rap verses; hooks SUNG alto to soprano, Lauryn
Hill's soul power.

Album mood: depth, patience, pressure — the long game. Detroit
strait water: the river that runs between two countries and
keeps everything it is given.
