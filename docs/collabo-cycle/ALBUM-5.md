# ALBUM V — THIRTEEN

The Five-Album Collabo Cycle, final volume. The whole 13-fold
engine on one record: nine members in seat order, three crews
combined, and the Zero held for the founder's own hand.

GHOST-WRITING DRAFTS — the crews' own bars; Qasparr & Qveen Bee
features ghost-drafted, marked; DRAFT until the founder stamps.

Formation law (founder's ruling): the BODY of every song is the
crew member's own bars, written and carried by the member in
their own voice and seat. QASPARR and QVEEN BEE FEATURE — his
feature verse, her feature hook or verse — each marked
[GHOST DRAFT for Qasparr] / [GHOST DRAFT for Qveen Bee]. When
the founder writes his own feature bars himself, they replace
the ghost draft byte for byte.
