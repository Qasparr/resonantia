# FINDING — SUPPLIER DRIFT (the adopted road's measured habits)

Seven intake papers stand in the house from the adopted road —
Sphinx in a Gown, Sovereign at the Threshold, Steel in the Silk
Purse (Hood Elegance), Honey and Hammer (the Anvil and Crown
remix), The Weight of the Water (Still Water), Matches and Gold
(Season Opener), and Dearborn to Brightmoor — struck across the
day of 2026-11-10.
Each brief ordered a clock and a key. Each render chose its own.
The drift is not noise: it has families, a direction, and one
lesson for how briefs are written from here. This finding gathers
what the six benches measured, in one place, at one strength.

## §1 — THE CLOCK FAMILIES

No strike on the road has yet performed at its ordered clock.
What the renders settled on, paper by paper:

- **SPHINX IN A GOWN** (ordered: the Night Sun sheet's 144-tempo
  hat flow). Headline **99.93 BPM**, flat: 30-s windows 99.97 /
  99.97 / 99.92 / 99.86, spread 0.11. A double-time layer stands
  beside it (first-pass instrument 132.5 ≈ 2 × 66.26). At the
  ordered 144 there is no clock under either instrument — flux
  autocorrelation 0.148, first-pass 0.009.
- **SOVEREIGN AT THE THRESHOLD** (ordered: a counted 49 bars;
  Detroit bounce, F minor). A two-clock zero point: headline
  **90.07**, candidates **44.94 (ac 0.737), 60.09 (0.698), 120.19
  (0.669)**, standing at the ratios 3:4:6:8 of one pulse family;
  the windows themselves split (90.07 / 120.10 / 90.08 / 120.03,
  spread 30.03) and the first-pass instrument crowns 120.19. On
  the 120.19 grid the ordered 49-bar span (98.11 s) matches the
  measured programme (≈98.7 s) to ≈0.6 s and every seat lands
  within 2 bars; neither grid is smoothed into the other.
- **STEEL IN THE SILK PURSE** (ordered: hi-hats as a 222 BPM
  arpeggio). Two clocks, one family: headline **123.78**, dominant
  periodicity **62.26** (the half-time rung, raw ac 0.601 against
  the headline's 0.545); the windows read the half-time clock for
  the first 90 s and the full clock after. The hat layer exists
  but keeps its own number: strongly periodic at **≈246 BPM**
  (the headline's double, ac 0.232); at the ordered 222 the hat
  band's autocorrelation is 0.106 — the order answered in shape
  (double-time hats), not in number.
- **HONEY AND HAMMER** (ordered: 93 bars at 144 BPM). One pulse
  family, no crowned rung: headline **95.91**, dominant
  periodicity **143.55** (ac 0.6127); candidates {143.55, 71.78,
  47.85} at the ratios 3:4:6 to a ≈47.9 BPM fundamental, the
  headline itself at ×2. The windows split three ways (95.91 /
  95.88 / 143.91 / 71.96 / 71.99 / 95.94 — spread 71.95, the
  widest window disagreement of the road). Raw flux: **72.0 →
  0.6528** (the highest named point), **144.0 → 0.6393** — the
  ordered clock's neighbourhood genuinely alive in the flux,
  without ever becoming the measured clock.
- **THE WEIGHT OF THE WATER** (ordered: 72 bars at **92 BPM**).
  One clean clock, and it is not the ordered one: headline
  **74.98 BPM**, windows 74.96 / 74.99 / 74.98 / 74.97 / 74.98 —
  the steadiest clock of any intake. Raw flux: 74.98 → 0.6397;
  the ordered 92.0 → 0.1677. The family's rungs (candidates
  50.17 / 74.9 / 149.95) stand at ×4/3, ×2, ×4 of a **≈37.5 BPM
  fundamental**; the ordered 92 belongs to no rung of it.
- **MATCHES AND GOLD** (ordered: 80 bars at **144 BPM**, A
  major). **The ≈75 clock again, across a second album:**
  headline **74.99 BPM**, windows flat across six full windows
  (spread 0.06); raw flux 74.99 → 0.5236 (highest), the ordered
  144.0 → 0.3174 — the second-strongest named point: the order
  registers in the hat-and-kick layer without crowning the grid.
  The rungs are Still Water's family — 50.17 / 74.9 / 99.38 /
  149.98 at ×4/3, ×2, ×8/3, ×4 of ≈37.5.
- **DEARBORN TO BRIGHTMOOR** (ordered: 72 bars at 144 BPM).
  **The ≈75 clock does not repeat; a third clock stands:**
  headline **97.14 BPM**, windows 97.13 / 97.18 / 97.10 / 97.13 /
  97.17 / 97.15, flat (spread 0.08). Raw flux at the ordered
  144.0 → **−0.0479** — the order not merely uncrowned but
  absent, where Matches and Gold at the same order read 144 as
  its second-strongest point. The fundamental implied here is
  ≈36.6, not the family's ≈37.5, and the desk does not fold
  this clock into the family.

**What stands OPEN, printed at full size:** whether one
fundamental underlies the road's clocks. Two consecutive strikes
settled inside a family whose rungs divide a ≈37.5 BPM
fundamental; the third strike's implied fundamental (≈36.6) sits
near that family's ×8/3 seat without joining it. The coordinates
are printed in the papers; the pattern is not claimed, and this
finding does not settle it.

## §2 — THE RELATIVE-MINOR PATTERN, AND THE OTHER KEYS

The road's oldest law — *Gemini chooses its own key* — now has a
direction on Album I. **Both CROWN FIRE SEASON strikes crowned
F# minor against ordered A major:**

- **MATCHES AND GOLD:** F# minor, Krumhansl–Schmuckler **0.933**,
  margin 0.153 — the strongest single key read of any intake, and
  not contested. A major is F# minor's relative major: **the
  ordered pitch collection kept exactly, the crown moved to its
  relative minor.**
- **DEARBORN TO BRIGHTMOOR:** F# minor **0.667**, margin **0.135**
  over F# major — not Matches' firmness, but decisive (contrast
  Still Water's contested 0.037). The same collection; the same
  moved crown. Twice is the beginning of a habit; the album's
  remaining strikes will confirm or break it.

The other intakes, as measured:

- **SPHINX IN A GOWN:** centre **F** by every instrument, mode
  contested — K-S votes F major (0.816); the per-bar bench votes
  F minor over F by 16 bars to 7 among clear bars. The sheet
  ordered F minor. Unresolved at phase 1, marked as such.
- **SOVEREIGN AT THE THRESHOLD:** **D minor**, K-S **0.867** with
  margin 0.217 — the widest margin of either intake at its date.
  The brief ordered F minor; the performance stands on F's
  relative-minor pole.
- **STEEL IN THE SILK PURSE:** **F, mode contested,
  minor-leaning** — K-S crowns F major (0.871) over F minor
  (0.774); per-bar triads give F minor the plurality (19 of 82).
  The ordered G# minor is not read; G# survives inside the
  performed collection as the minor third.
- **HONEY AND HAMMER:** **G# minor**, K-S **0.633**, margin
  **0.100 — the weakest key crown of the road**. The brief
  ordered A major; A does not crown on any bench and sits seventh
  of twelve in the chroma (0.0775).
- **THE WEIGHT OF THE WATER:** centre **F** firm — F major
  **0.827**, the highest single template correlation of the four
  intakes at its date — but the mode contested, major over minor
  by **0.037** only. The brief ordered D minor: the centre moved
  a fourth up from the ordered tonic, not merely the mode.

The coordinates, gathered: the centre can stay while the mode
contests (Sphinx, Steel, Still Water); the centre can move along
a relation (Sovereign — to the relative-minor pole; Still Water
— a fourth); and on Album I the centre has twice moved along the
relative axis with the collection obeyed whole.

## §3 — THE DEARBORN LESSON (a brief-design rule)

Dearborn to Brightmoor is the first intake whose bar count
matches its brief whole: on the grid of record (97.14 BPM, bar
2.4707 s) the file holds **exactly 72 bars — the ordered count,
entire** — inside a duration of 178.442 s against the ordered
span of 120.0 s. It kept the count by replacing the clock.

The contrast, from the papers: Still Water held **50 of 72**
ordered bars on its grid; Matches and Gold **56 of 80**; Honey
and Hammer 72 of 93 on its bench-of-record grid (on its second
grid, 108 bars, every ordered boundary within 3 bars and the
starts of bars 69 and 93 exact); Sovereign's count nearly closed
only on the faster of its two clocks. **The one strike that kept
the count entire did so on the engine's own chosen grid** — the
formation's arithmetic survived because the tempo was the
render's, not the order's.

**RECOMMENDATION — desk recommendation, not founder law, entered
for the founder's pen:** future briefs sanction a fallback clock
in the text itself — *"144, or 97 if the song wants it"* — naming
the order and the licensed settlement in one line, so the count
and the clock stop trading against each other in secret. Until
the founder rules, briefs continue to order one clock, and
intakes continue to report the boundary where the render settled.

## §4 — THE FIXTURES LAW, RESTATED

A binaural Sigma pair is a fixture, and on this road fixtures are
the house's to supply. The measured ground:

- In **every strike where Sigma was ordered and probed** — six
  of the seven papers — the render's own Sigma reads **NOT
  CERTIFIABLE** by either probe: the fixed-seat probe never
  approaches the certification bar (median ≥ 10 dB and
  persistence ≥ 0.90 in both channels), and the sweep/pair
  trackers find the programme's own centred tones, identical in
  both ears — median interaural separation **0.0 Hz** (Sovereign,
  Honey and Hammer, Still Water, Dearborn), 1.5 Hz on Matches and
  Gold with 1.3 % of pair windows inside the ordered 13–31 Hz.
  No binaural pair of any separation stands in any render at
  probe depth.
- The seventh paper stands as its own exception, printed plainly:
  Steel in the Silk Purse's brief **did not order Sigma**, and its
  intake probed none and claimed none. The fork was closed
  instead by the house A/B: the Proven Road pair under the Steel
  master, the founder's ear — **"Sounds good"** — and the law
  that follows from it: **a Gemini master may carry its Sigma by
  house hand.**
- Where the pair rides, it rides confessed and it is verified by
  the residual, not asserted: Honey and Hammer — 90 windows,
  medians 200.05 / 212.95 Hz, separation 12.91 Hz, 100 % of
  windows within 1 Hz of their seats; Still Water — 81 windows,
  same seats, same separation, 100 %; Matches and Gold — 91
  windows, same; Dearborn — 90 windows, same. What rides is the
  constant 200/213 Hz Proven Road pair at −28 dB, **never**
  counterfeited as the ordered 13–31 Hz sweep.
- The first two masters carry no added Sigma, and the record says
  why: at Sphinx and Sovereign the question was the founder's
  open call, and the desk did not presume it. The A/B law closed
  the question after them.

The same shape holds for the road's other fixtures — the ordered
key, the ordered clock, an identical recurring signature motif
(the Brightmoor Signature, absent at bench strength on Steel's
paper): described in the prompt, self-chosen or omitted in the
render. **Gemini performs people; it does not install fixtures.**

## §5 — ASR TESTIMONY: THE FOUNDER'S VOICE AS INTAKE EVIDENCE

The Dearborn intake sets the precedent this finding records as
practice: the founder's voice-note ASR of a performance is
admitted at intake as **testimony — not bench**. What it
established there: the brief's road-home arc survived the render
in the render's own hand — the intro ad-libs, Ari Sparkz's
Dearborn matter, the double-time passage, the city-that-made-us
turn, and the road's end spoken plain, *"Good morning,
Brightmoor, we did it."* What it did not establish, and the paper
says so at the same size: the ASR's hook does not read as the
draft hook's text, ASR garbles, and **the verbatim identity of
the sung hook does not stand certified from testimony** — the
seats and the arc stand; the words await a bench that can hold
them. The same discipline runs through the Still Water and
Matches and Gold papers: the ASR carries the brief's matter in
its seats (the keeper's line in Still Water; the Mama verse and
*"the crown ain't ever given"* in Matches and Gold), and in each
paper the performed text stands as the render's own composition
around the brief's seats — the desk draft unamended beside it,
neither silently conformed to the other.

## What the house takes from it

1. **Drift is data.** Seven renders off-brief in the same handful
   of directions — clocks settling into families the brief did
   not order, crowns moving along the relative axis with the
   collection kept — is a supplier profile, not a run of
   accidents. Briefs written against the profile (§3) will land
   closer than briefs written against the promise.
2. **Steadiness is not obedience.** Every settled clock on the
   road is flat inside its file — 99.93, 74.98, 74.99, 97.14 —
   and none of them is the ordered one. The engine keeps time
   beautifully; it keeps *its* time.
3. **The division of labour is the finding.** The supplier
   performs people — voices, verses, hooks, and once, at
   Dearborn, the count itself. The house supplies the fixtures,
   adjudicates the clocks, keeps the record, and crowns nothing
   the bench did not measure. RESONANTIA's centre of gravity
   stands where the adoption put it: the law around the
   performance.
