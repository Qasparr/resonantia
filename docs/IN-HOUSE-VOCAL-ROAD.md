# THE IN-HOUSE VOCAL ROAD — the house's own singing voice

The short answer is yes, and the road has three layers. Two of
them are engineering. One of them is an asset. None of them is
a research programme, and none of them starts from zero.

## Layer 1 — THE ENGINE: already open, already proven

The house does not need to invent a singing synthesizer.
DiffSinger (OpenVPI, Apache 2.0) is a score-first singing
engine — score + lyrics in, a sung vocal out — that runs
locally on CPU, and it has already sung in this house: the
first self-sufficient run (Spear and Magickal Helm, 2026-11-15)
carried its hook and a verse chant end-to-end. What remains is
not invention but integration: a Resonantia module on the
MusicGen bridge pattern — score + lyrics in, sung WAV out,
manifest measured — with the Divine Nine seated at the ph_seq
plug point as the canonical vowel interlingua, exactly as
designed in docs/THE-DIVINE-NINE-VOCAL-TOOL.md. Buildable on
this box, at no cost but desk time. Proof target for the
phase: the DETROIT MUGETSU hook rendered end-to-end through
the engine itself, not the lab.

## Layer 2 — THE VOICE: the asset, and the real gate

Yamaha's launch prices the lesson: the editor is $129 and the
banks are the catalogue. A singing engine is a tool; a
voicebank is the asset. The house's own bank is trainable
through MakeDiffSinger (BSD-3) — a phoneme-labelled, sung
dataset in, a bank out, licence-clean by birth because the
house made it. Its gates are physical, not conceptual:
(a) a dataset — on the order of an hour of clean,
phoneme-balanced sung recordings, Japanese and English;
(b) training compute — a GPU this box does not have, which is
the workstation already specced for the owned road (24 GB
NVIDIA class); (c) a voice source that is ours by right — a
commissioned or licensed singer, or a synthetic corpus. House
law binds the source absolutely: never the founder's recorded
voice (he declines recording), never a clone of any person.
Until the bank exists, Lem the Shapeshifter carries the seat
under its own TOS, named as borrowed wherever it sings.

## Layer 3 — THE CRAFT: study the leaders, copy nothing

VOCALOID7's new craft — ATTACK articulation control, VOICE
TYPE switching, pitch-curve preservation — is Yamaha's
product, and its code stays Yamaha's. The ideas are free to
study, and their house equivalents live where they belong: in
the score layer, as performance direction written into the
house's own notation and plug design. Clean-room only: ideas
in, no code across.

## What this road changes, and what it does not

It does not change today: Gemini still sings the production
strikes by adoption, VOCALOID7 and ACE stay filed for the day
a Windows or Mac machine enters the house, and ZONOS3-Mini
holds the speech seat. What the road changes is the ending:
every other seat on the bench is somebody else's engine or
somebody else's voice. This is the only line on the bench
where both are the house's.

## Order of work

- **Phase 1 — Integration** (desk, this box, no purchase):
  the DiffSinger module in the engine, the Nine at the plug
  point, the Mugetsu hook as the phase proof.
- **Phase 2 — The house bank** (gated: the workstation, and
  a voice source ruled by the founder): dataset built to
  spec, bank trained, licence born clean.
- **Phase 3 — The craft** (continuous): articulation and
  voice-character direction in the score layer, studied from
  the leaders, written in the house's own hand.
