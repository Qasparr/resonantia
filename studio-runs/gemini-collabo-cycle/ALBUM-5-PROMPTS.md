the hooks. Dark grand piano. A binaural Sigma layer embedded,
sweeping 13–31 Hz. THE KEY CLIMBS THE ALBUM: Tracks 1–4 in
F minor · Tracks 5–8 in G# minor · Tracks 9–12 in A major.
