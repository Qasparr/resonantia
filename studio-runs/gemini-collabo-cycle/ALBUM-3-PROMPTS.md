# ALBUM III — FEATURED VOICES · GEMINI STRIKE PROMPT PACK

**Album:** ALBUM III — FEATURED VOICES (Five-Album Collabo Cycle, Album 3 of 5)
**Lead crew:** THE FEATURED VOICES — Crew III, the Air Triad (WORD · BALANCE · SIGNAL)
**Headliner:** QVEEN BEE — this is her album of the cycle. Qasparr features.
**Lyric source:** `docs/collabo-cycle/ALBUM-3.md` — paste the STAMPED text of each
track into that track's lyric slot before striking. Drafts are not struck; only
the founder-stamped text goes in the slot.

## ALBUM PRODUCTION SPEC (carried in full inside every block)

- **Genre / bounce:** Detroit bounce trap.
- **Tempo / key:** 144 BPM · G# minor — every track, no drift.
- **Lead instrument:** GRAND PIANO. No lead violin anywhere on this album —
  the founder struck the violin out on this line. Strings, if any, sit low
  in the bed and never lead.
- **The Brightmoor Signature:** a dark four-note grand-piano motif. It opens
  EVERY track alone, before anything else sounds, and it answers EVERY hook —
  the same four notes, identical every time they play, including the last
  sound before the final stop.
- **Low end:** 808 super kick, deep and forward.
- **Hats:** Paganini hi-hat arpeggios — fast, ornate, virtuoso rolls.
- **Harmonica:** over selected hooks only (marked per track below).
- **Voices:** three female voices forward on every track — Qveen Bee,
  Lady Lilith, Banshee (per-track seats vary; the three-voice weave is the
  album's sound). Qasparr features where the formation calls for him.
- **Sigma layer:** an embedded binaural layer sweeping 13–31 Hz rides under
  every track, ordered in every block.

## VOICE DIRECTION (album-wide)

- **QVEEN BEE** — HEADLINER. Female rap voice, sultry and confident, Eve's
  flow with Da Brat's linguistic density; twin-tongued double-time when the
  part is marked. Her hooks are SUNG, alto rising to soprano, with soul
  power — Lauryn Hill weight, lifted bright and high.
- **LADY LILITH, THE MOTHER TONGUE** — female voice, measured and weighted;
  hooks delivered like verdicts — slow, certain, final. The first voice and
  the last word.
- **BANSHEE** — female soprano, ethereal wail, high frequencies no one else
  on the label reaches; the warning and the wonder in one note.
- **QASPARR** (features only) — medium-pitch male rap, forward and urgent,
  crystal-clear diction, emotion at the front (the 2Pac register).

## FORMATION LAW (founder's refinement, 2026-11-10)

The body of every song is the Featured Voices' OWN bars, written and
carried by the members themselves. Qveen Bee headlines. Qasparr FEATURES —
his verse or bridge is the crown on the crew's work, not the body of it.
