## ALBUM PRODUCTION SPEC (carried in every block below)

- **Sound:** grounded Detroit bounce — heavy, weight-first, built
  to stand on. Heavy kick with real weight in the chest, live-feel
  drums with room in the kit, deep sub bass that moves like
  groundwater, grand piano, Hammond-style organ, and gospel choir
  pads that swell under the closers. Nothing floats away on this
  album: every track is a structure being raised on Detroit ground.
- **Tempo / key (locked, every track):** 103 BPM, F minor — the
  Brightmoor Rachets tempo and key.
- **Sigma (ordered in every block):** a binaural Sigma sweep,
  13 to 31 Hz, embedded under the whole performance, first bar to
  last, felt more than heard.
- **FORMATION LAW (founder's ruling):** the BODY of every song is
  the crews' own bars, in their own voices and seats. QASPARR and
  QVEEN BEE FEATURE where the tracklist seats them — his feature
  verse, her feature hook or verse. The crown sits on the crews'
  work; it is not the body of it.

## HOW TO STRIKE

1. ONE song per Gemini prompt. Copy ONE fenced block below,
   whole, into Gemini — never two tracks in one prompt.
2. Before striking, paste the STAMPED lyric text into the block's
   LYRICS slot: take Track N from `docs/collabo-cycle/ALBUM-4.md`,
   founder-stamped text only, and drop each part under its label
   ([HOOK], [VERSE — name], etc.). The slots below carry the part
   labels and the form; the words come from the stamped sheet.
   If a track's text is not stamped yet, the block waits — do not
   strike a draft.
3. Strike in album order, 1 → 9. Upload each returned mp3 to the
   desk for intake (measure, master to house law, notate) before
   moving on.
4. Voice signatures are written into every block — do not
   paraphrase them away when copying.
