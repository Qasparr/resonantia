# ALBUM I — CROWN FIRE SEASON
## Gemini strike prompt pack · Five-Album Collabo Cycle

**Crew:** CROWN FIRE (Crew I — the Fire Triad: IGNITE · BLAZE · TRAVEL)
**Headliners featuring:** QASPARR & QVEEN BEE

### Album production spec (carried into every block below)

- Detroit bounce trap, **144 BPM**, **A major**, all nine tracks.
- 808 super kick; Paganini-arpeggio hi-hats at speed; tom drums; harmonica riding over the hooks; grand piano accents.
- **Sigma layer embedded:** a binaural layer sweeping 13–31 Hz under the whole track (left/right slightly detuned tones), felt more than heard.
- Voices: **Qasparr** — medium-pitch male rap, forward and urgent, slight nasal edge, crystal-clear diction, emotion at the front (2Pac register). **Qveen Bee** — female, sultry confident rap flow; hooks SUNG alto to soprano with soul power. **Sol Rey** — male, anthem captain, big chant-ready delivery. **Ari Sparkz** — male, percussive double-time flow. **Sage Archer** — female, sung hooks with range, warm and far-carrying.
- Formation law: the crew's own bars are the body of every song; Qasparr and Qveen Bee FEATURE (his verse, her hook or verse) as the tracklist assigns.

### How to strike

Strike **one prompt per Gemini session, in track order, 1 through 9**. Copy the whole fenced block into the Gemini app exactly as written, with the stamped lyric text pasted into the LYRICS slot inside the block (lyrics: `docs/collabo-cycle/ALBUM-1.md`, stamped track text). Export the audio, name it by track number and title, and hand it to the desk for intake and mastering.
