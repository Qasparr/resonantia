Sub-bass pressure you feel in the sternum, sparse dark grand piano, low strings, brushed toms, wide wet room — space to drown in. Nothing hurried. The Sigma layer is ordered embedded in every track: a low binaural tone pair beating at **13 Hz**, slow and constant, felt more than heard.

**VOICES:**
- **QASPARR** (feature) — male rap, medium pitch, forward and urgent, crystal diction, emotion at the front (2Pac register) — on this album: lower, closer, under the surface, like a voice through water.
- **QVEEN BEE** (feature) — female, sultry and alluring rap flow (Eve's flow, Da Brat's linguistics); hooks SUNG, alto rising to soprano (Lauryn Hill's soul power lifted bright).
- **NOIR** (crew, Scorpio — VENOM) — female, quiet, measured, dangerous: near-whisper verses that cut, every line double-bottomed.
- **LULLABY LUX** (crew, Pisces — DEPTH) — female, melodic: sings hooks like a tide coming in, melodies that surface in your head at 3 a.m.
- **HARBOR JUNE** (crew, Cancer — MEMORY) — male, warm storyteller baritone, names-and-dates delivery, verses like a family Bible read aloud.

**FORMATION LAW:** The crews' own bars are the body of every song. Qasparr and Qveen Bee FEATURE — his feature verse, her feature hook or verse — per the tracklist. Guests visit; seats don't move.

**HARMONICA:** A distant harmonica wail rides on exactly two tracks of this album — **Track 2 "3 A.M. Tide"** and **Track 8 "Moon over the Strait"** (the two night-water tracks; the wail is a sound heard across the strait, far off, never close). No harmonica on any other track.
