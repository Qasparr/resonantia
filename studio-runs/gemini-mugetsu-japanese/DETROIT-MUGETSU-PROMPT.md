# DETROIT MUGETSU — 『デトロイト無月』
## Gemini strike prompt pack · single-song package · MUGETSU-GUMI

**Crew:** MUGETSU-GUMI （無月組） — the Japanese detachment, formed 2026-11-12 by the founder's order for this one song: **KUROGANE** (male lead) · **YAMI** (female, the hook) · **SHION** (female, the invocation)
**Song:** 『デトロイト無月』 DETROIT MUGETSU — a full Japanese gangsta rap. Performance text JAPANESE ONLY.

### Song production spec (carried into the block below)

- Detroit bounce trap, **144 BPM**, **G# minor** (desk key choice — see the notation files: the song's family spine is the Unfriendly Ghost album's 144 BPM / G# minor seat, and the Brightmoor Signature's home pack, Album III, rides G# minor; the dark grand piano and the moonless register sit in the minor).
- 808 super kick; Paganini-arpeggio hi-hats at speed; tom drums; **dark grand piano lead**; the **Brightmoor Signature**: a dark four-note grand-piano motif (G#–E–D#–B), identical wherever it plays — it opens the track alone, answers the hook, and is the last piano sound before the stop.
- **Sigma layer ordered:** a binaural layer sweeping 13–31 Hz under the whole track. House law rides with the order: the binaural pair is a fixture the desk installs at mastering (the Proven Road pair, per the closed Sigma A/B law) — Gemini performs the song; the house installs the pair.
- Voices — REGISTER specs only; no real person's voice or likeness is asked for or used (house law):
  - **KUROGANE** (lead) — young male rap in Japanese: medium pitch, forward and urgent, rough edge, slight nasal bite, crystal-clear diction, emotion at the front. A voice that testifies, not booms — the testimony seat.
  - **YAMI** (hook) — female, low alto, cool and level, dark warmth; she sings the hook like a verdict, no ornament wasted.
  - **SHION** (invocation) — female soprano, clear and high, bell-clean; she carries the outro chant slow, over silence.
- Formation for this song: Kurogane leads every verse; Yami sings the hook; Shion carries the outro invocation alone.
