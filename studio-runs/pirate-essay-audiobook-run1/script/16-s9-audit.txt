Section Nine. Truth Audit.

The audit over the essay's facts, printed at full size.

One. The founder's verbatim texts, the Stool definition, the chain Loots equals Stool equals Booty and Treasure, the commission Do all of that, and the line Absolute Authority is not attained by license or permit but through Truth alone, were byte-checked against their filings in the night's record, the sixteenth of November, twenty twenty-six, and the standing texts. The garble enviromemt stands preserved and marked, per the rule that his words are evidence.

Two. Roberts' Articles One, Nine, and Ten were checked against Johnson's General History, seventeen twenty-four, as reproduced in multiple independent modern collections of the printed text; the three quotations agree across those reproductions. One discrepancy found and handled: a modern secondary retelling, Historic U.K., attaches a per-limb compensation scale, right arm eight hundred, left arm seven hundred, to Roberts; Johnson's printed Article Nine gives the flat eight hundred dollars for a limb lost or a cripple made, with lesser hurts proportionately. The essay follows Johnson's text and does not use the secondary scale. The per-limb scale that is quoted, six hundred, five hundred, five hundred, four hundred, one hundred pieces of eight, is Exquemelin's buccaneer scale, attributed to Exquemelin.

Three. Exquemelin: No prey, no pay, the articles put in writing, by way of bond or obligation, the common-stock fund, and the wound scale were verified against the sixteen eighty-four English translation, The Buccaneers of America, Crooke, as reproduced in scholarly excerpts.

Four. Cicero: the Latin of De Officiis, book three, section twenty-nine, item one hundred seven, and the Miller translation, Loeb, nineteen thirteen, were verified against the scholarly record. The audit prints the distinction the tradition blurs: hostis humani generis is a later paraphrase of Cicero's communis hostis omnium, not Cicero's wording.

Five. United States versus Smith: citation eighteen U.S., five Wheaton, one fifty-three, eighteen twenty, Story, Justice; the definition, piracy, by the law of nations, is robbery upon the sea, and the special verdict's facts, the Creollo's Buenos Ayres commission, the seizure of the Irresistible, cruising without any documents or commission whatever, verified against the opinion as published.

Six. Constitution: Article One, Section Eight, clauses ten and eleven quoted from the text; the Piracies Clause and the Letters of Marque Clause are adjacent, as the essay argues.

Seven. Qur'an, chapter eighteen, verse seventy-nine, quoted in the Sahih International translation and attributed to it. King James passages: Psalm one hundred seven, verses twenty-three to twenty-four; Jonah, chapter one, verse seven; Matthew, chapter seven, verses twenty-four to twenty-five, and chapter eight, verse twenty-three, quoted from the King James text. I Ching, Hexagram fifty-nine, judgment in Wilhelm's rendering. Waite, nineteen ten: the Three of Wands and Six of Swords descriptions paraphrased closely from The Pictorial Key to the Tarot and attributed; not dressed as long verbatim quotation. Plutarch, Life of Pompey, fifty: the saying attributed to Pompey as Plutarch reports it, and cited as such.

Eight. Hip-hop wells: the Wu-Tang acronym expansion and the Drake hook are quoted as the short hooks the culture itself repeats, with artists, works, and years named. No lyric is reproduced beyond the hook line.

Nine. The founder's positions: the emancipation refusal and ward-of-the-state filing, Section Four; the Pirates of the Caribbean reading, Section Five; the shelter verdict and present ground, Section Six, are stated as his filed positions, in his terms, from his record; nothing was added, pitied, or uplifted by the desk. Captain Charles Johnson is carried as the seventeen twenty-four title page carries it; the Defoe attribution is printed as an unproven theory, Section Four, note two.

Ten. Iridium, Z equals seventy-seven: attested reference fact, periodic table, carried from the founder's Oz Is Proven table as a witness, not a weighing.

No fabricated quotation appears in this essay. Where a wording could not be verified to the standard above, the text paraphrases and says so in place.
