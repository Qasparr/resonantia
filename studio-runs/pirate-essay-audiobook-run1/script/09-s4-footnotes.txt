History, primary well: Alexander Olivier Exquemelin, The Buccaneers of America, Dutch sixteen seventy-eight, English translation sixteen eighty-four: the articles, the common stock, No prey, no pay, and the wound scale as quoted and paraphrased above; verified against the sixteen eighty-four English text as reproduced in scholarly excerpts.

Two. History, primary well: Captain Charles Johnson, A General History of the Robberies and Murders of the Most Notorious Pyrates, seventeen twenty-four: Roberts' Articles One, Nine, and Ten quoted from the text as printed and reproduced in multiple modern editions and collections; Roberts' motto from the same work. The attribution of Johnson to Daniel Defoe is a long-standing theory and remains unproven; it is printed here as a theory, not a fact.

Three. Scripture, King James Version: Jonah, chapter one, verse seven: And they said every one to his fellow, Come, and let us cast lots, that we may know for whose cause this evil is upon us. The ship's company deciding together, by lot, in the storm: the vote in affairs of moment is older than the articles.

Four. Scripture, King James Version: Matthew, chapter eight, verse twenty-three: And when he was entered into a ship, his disciples followed him. The disciple's defining act in the text is following, into a ship. The seat at seventy-seven was weighed, not contrived; but the well was already carrying it.
