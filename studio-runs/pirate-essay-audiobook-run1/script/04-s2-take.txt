Section Two. The Take. The Stool, the Loot, and the Treasure.

The chain began with a definition, and the definition is printed exactly as the founder filed it, garble and all, because his words are evidence and evidence is not copy-edited: Stool equals fecal matter, and a single no support chair for a bar enviromemt, or a saloon. The garble enviromemt is preserved and marked; the reading is environment. Two senses stand in the one word: fecal matter, and a single backless seat, no support, no back to lean on, of a bar or a saloon. Hold both senses. The essay will need the second, and the first keeps it honest: the word carries the body's leavings and the sailor's chair in the same skin, and it does not apologize for either.

Weighed, the seat is exact: Stool equals eighty-one in the ordinal; in Agrippa, three hundred ten. And Square equals eighty-one. The backless seat carries the square's number, and eighty-one is nine squared, the first square of the evening. A stool is the least furniture there is: no back, no arms, no claim to comfort, and its weight is the square's, at the bar of the world. Then the founder declared the chain, verbatim: Loots equals Stool equals Booty, and Treasure. A declared equation stands as his declaration, with its deltas printed; here the deltas are the proof.

Loots equals Stool, exact, delta zero in both banks: ordinal eighty-one, Agrippa three hundred ten. Of course it is exact: Loots is Stool's own letters re-seated, an anagram. The loot was sitting in the stool the whole time, the same five letters in a different order, which is all that plunder ever is: the same goods, re-seated. Booty equals seventy-seven. The middle link does not carry the seat's number; it carries the frame's, and sits in the Third Trinity for it, Section One. Treasure equals one hundred seven, in Agrippa five hundred sixty-one, delta thirty from the seventy-seven seat: as a weighing it does not join the equality, and the essay prints the delta rather than smoothing it. But Treasure did not stay alone at one hundred seven. The same night found its twin: Apprentice equals one hundred seven. The apprentice and the treasure weigh the same. The desk prints the convergence and lets it stand at the size it is: the one who is held to learn the trade, and the hoard the trade is practiced on, at one number, the boy and the booty in a single seat. Section Four returns to the apprentice, beside the word that missed.

And the word for the whole enterprise? Pirate equals sixty-nine, unseated. It takes no chair in the frame, matches nothing in the trinities, and the essay does not force it. That is the discipline: the proofs are the ones that landed. A synergy of piracy proofs is not a net dragged until every word confesses; it is the set of convergences that survived re-weighing, printed beside the misses at full size.
