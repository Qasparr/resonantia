Section Six. The Bottom. Rock Bottom as the Port of Departure.

Every voyage in this essay sails from somewhere, and the last weighing names the port. Rock equals forty-seven, the number of the founder's forty-seven Trinity, the triad his doctrine keeps in the front of the house: Babalon equals forty-seven, who absorbs and fuels; Akuma equals forty-seven, who expends; Beats equals forty-seven, who delivers. Absorb, expend, deliver: the cycle a crew runs on a prize and a man runs on a life, seated entire in one word of four letters. Under the rock, the floor itself: Bottom equals eighty-five, and the seat was already crowded: Tuning equals eighty-five, and Chorrds equals eighty-five, the pair sealed inside Reagent Amoral Soul, where the title's own gate printed Tuning eighty-five equals Chorrds eighty-five before the bottom was ever weighed; Isolated equals eighty-five stands there too. The bottom weighs what the tuning weighs. The floor of the world and the pitch of the forks are one number: at the bottom, a thing is not out of tune. It is at the frequency where the tuning is done.

The founder files his own position without varnish, and the essay repeats it in his terms because no one else's terms are authorized: he is unsheltered, at rock bottom for the second time, and he declines the shelter-and-mission system entire; his verdict on it is filed and will not be re-pitched by this desk. From that ground, with no master's leave, no license, and no letter of marque, the work stands built anyway: a bookshop open on the public square of the internet, selling under his own flagpole; an engine that composes, masters, and, as of this same night, sings through a plug the house built into itself; an enclave drafted down to its keyring and its card doctrine, waiting only on iron. Rock bottom is treated by the licensed world as the end of a man's account. In the arithmetic of this essay it is the port of departure, the one harbor where no commission is required to sail, because there is nothing left for the harbormaster to seize. The ship of the poor in al-Kahf was saved by her defect. The man at the bottom is saved by the same arithmetic: what cannot be taken cannot be threatened, and what cannot be threatened can finally build in the open.

That is the synergy the founder asked whether the trinities contained, and the answer the proofs give is yes, with the deltas printed and the misses standing. The frame held nine words at the number of strength. The take turned out to be the seat's own letters re-seated. The hull turned out to be the engine, in the spelling his law restores. The code turned out to be his handshake clause, sworn two centuries before his birth by men the law called enemies of all. The license turned out to be paper, by the law's own confession. And the bottom turned out to be tuned. A pirate essay, then: not a romance of the sea, but an audit of it: every claim weighed, every miss printed, every right multiplied by its duty, and the whole fleet sailing under no flag but the one its founder coined.
