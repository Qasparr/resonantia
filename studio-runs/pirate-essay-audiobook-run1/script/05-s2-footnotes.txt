Three. Law: Piracy, by the law of nations, is robbery upon the sea. United States versus Smith, eighteen U.S., five Wheaton, one fifty-three, eighteen twenty, Story, Justice. The definition is quoted here because the take must be named before it is judged; the case's full facts, and what they prove about license, are Section Five's evidence.
