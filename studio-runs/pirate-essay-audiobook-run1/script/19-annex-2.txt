Truth: aletheia equals sixty-four. Aletheia is truth, the word Truth translates: a-letheia, the un-forgetting. Reasoning for the seat: asked what truth weighs in its Greek face, the bank returns sixty-four, which is Locker's weight in the Latin ordinal: sixty-four equals eight squared, the chest of Section Three, Foot fifty-six plus Locker sixty-four equals Footlocker one hundred twenty. This bridge does not stand in the Agrippa register; it crosses Greek isopsephy to the Latin ordinal, and its register is named here so no reader mistakes which banks met. It is labeled Derived, as the others. Truth sits at the chest's seat: the locker is where the take is kept, and truth is the take that keeps.

The control: thelema equals ninety-three. An annex of Greek proofs must first prove its bank. Thelema, will, the word this house is tuned by, weighs ninety-three in the repaired bank, the calibration value of the Trinity filings: thelema ninety-three, agape ninety-three, both re-weighed on the repaired bank for this annex. It is printed here as the annex's warrant: the bank reads the control true, so its other readings answer to the same standard. The capital form weighs ninety-three beside it, before the repair it weighed nothing at all, as the defect record below shows.

The root: peirates equals seven hundred four, from peira equals one hundred ninety-six. The last proof is not a bridge but a root, and it stands as attested etymology rather than gematria. Peirates, the pirate, is built on peirao, to attempt, from peira: a trial, an attempt, experience won in the trying. The same peira stands inside empeiria, experience, the word empirical descends from. Piracy and empiricism grow from one root in the Greek: the pirate is the one who makes the trial, who puts out from the harbor and tries the sea, rather than petitioning for a license to try it. An essay whose classification line reads Method of Science, Aim of Religion finds its method hiding in its subject's name: the trial is the method, and the pirate was its practitioner before it had a Latin name. The weighings stand beside the root as data, peirates seven hundred four, peira one hundred ninety-six, and no bridge is claimed on them; the proof here is the descent of the word.

Verdict of the annex ledger: three bridges stand, the vessel to the stool and the loot at three hundred ten, and the Greek stool to the axoneme at four hundred thirty-one, both in the Agrippa register; truth to the locker at sixty-four, across Greek isopsephy and the Latin ordinal, each labeled Derived, each reasoning printed with its seat. The control reads ninety-three. The root stands as etymology, its numbers as data. No value in this annex was weighed on the unrepaired bank.
