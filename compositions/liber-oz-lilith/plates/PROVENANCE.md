# Plates — Provenance

The three plates in this folder are one artwork in three frames: the
Author's **Axoneme mandala** — the zodiacal wheel over the heptagram,
the solfeggio ladder printed down both sides (UT 396, RE 417, MI 528,
FA 639, SOL 741, LA 852), the Fibonacci spiral (21 · 13 · 8 · 5), and
the seals AKUMA NO MINE / AXONEME and AGAPE / THELEMA — LOVE UNDER
WILL. The ladder, the spiral, and the chord doctrine of the Liber Oz
Lilith composition are all read off this plate; it is the
composition's visual source document.

- **Frontispiece** (`frontispiece-axoneme-mandala.png`, 1280×1952) —
  the portrait plate: the mandala crowned with the Tiara of Liberty,
  the golden chalice burning at its centre in purple and gold synergy
  flame, the ring lettered BINAURAL SIGMA FREQUENCY · 13 SACRED
  CHORDS · ARPEGGIO CYCLE.
- **Plate II** (`plate-ii-axoneme-mandala-16x9.png`, 2048×1152) — the
  same plate recomposed 16:9.
- **Plate III** (`plate-iii-axoneme-mandala-21x9.png`, 2352×1008) —
  the same plate recomposed 21:9.

## History of the plates (as recorded 2026-10-25)

The crown, chalice, and flames were added by an image edit at the
Author's dictation ("Add purple and golden flames on the avatar aura
field and chalice. And … the Tiara of Liberty Crown"), over his
original mandala artwork. The first edit **redrew** the mandala rather
than layering over it, and two of the ladder's numbers were corrupted
in the redraw (UT read 296 for 396; SOL read 541 for 741). A fidelity
pass, ordered by the Author the same evening, restored every figure:
the ladder in the shipped plates reads exactly 396 · 417 · 528 · 639 ·
741 · 852, and the spiral and titles stand true.

## Residual editor tics — disclosed, not hidden

The fidelity audit also recorded three residual tics of the image
editor, which stand in the plates as shipped and are disclosed here
exactly as reported:

1. the word **"key"** beneath each rung is rendered **"Bey"**;
2. **UT's label glyph is garbled** in the lower register;
3. the zodiacal wheel's **micro-lettering is impressionistic** rather
   than letter-exact.

These are surface tics of the redraw; the load-bearing figures — the
numbers the composition is built from — are exact, and they are the
figures the composition's own audits independently verify against the
engine's Solfeggio table.

## Conversion note

The plates were copied from `~/workspace/your_files/axoneme-mandala/`
(the Author's filed set, .webp) and converted to PNG for print by
lossless re-encode (PIL); no pixel was altered in the conversion.
16K wallpaper masters of Plates II and III remain in the filed set
and are not duplicated here.
