## 3. The formation audit — the ordered eleven against the measured fourteen

The brief ordered eleven sections: *intro (Signature alone, then
swell) → Qveen V1 → hook → Qveen V2 → hook → Qasparr V1 → Qasparr
V2 → bridge (halftime) → hook → tag-team final verse → Qveen sung
outro*. It ordered **no bar counts**, so this audit tests sequence
and content, not a census.

What the bench can honestly lay beside the order (INTERPRETATION,
from the table in §2):

- **The intro room stands.** Segment 0 (0–6.2 s, bars 1–3) is its
  own room: 13–15 dB below the body, rising slope (+2.79 dB/s),
  low centroid (1431 Hz against the body's ~4000), a low F-centred
  bass figure (see §4). Whether the ordered wind swell lives in it
  the bench cannot certify — but the shape (quiet, rising, alone at
  the head) is the intro's shape. **Confidence MEDIUM.**
- **A two-part loud body stands.** Segments 1–5 (6.2–62.4 s) and
  7–11 (77.9–143.9 s) form two loud masses at −14…−17 dB, with
  segment 1 a step quieter (−17.0) than what follows — consistent
  with a first verse settling before the first hook's full weight,
  but the segmenter split this body by texture, not by lyric
  section, and the three ordered hooks **cannot be isolated** in
  it. The recurrence web is dense (3↔6 at +0.973, 10→3 at +0.915,
  11→3 at +0.926): this performance repeats its rooms often, as a
  hook-driven song should — but which rooms are hooks, the bench
  cannot prove. **Confidence LOW for any finer attribution.**
- **One register dip stands at 62.4–77.9 s.** Segment 6 is the only
  body segment whose register median falls out of the 258–464 Hz
  cluster (223.9 Hz, female-rap-band) and the chroma twin of
  segment 3. A lower-centred rapped passage lives here — her
  double-time verse and his first verse are both candidates, and
  the bench cannot choose between them (see §5). **MEASURED as a
  dip; attribution LOW.**
- **The bridge's halftime dip is not separately visible.** No
  segment in the second body shows the level collapse a stripped
  halftime bridge would cut; if the bridge stands, it stands
  inside segments 7–11 unseamed. **Not located.**
- **The tag-team final verse is not separately visible** either —
  a line-for-line trade would not move the novelty instrument
  unless the texture changed with it. **Not located.**
- **The outro room stands, and it is the best voice evidence in
  the file.** Segment 12 (143.9–153.6 s) drops 12 dB below the
  body, its chroma stability falls to 0.46 (a lone line, not a
  cycling bed), and its voiced density is the file's highest
  (0.81) at a 375.3 Hz centre. That is the measured shape of one
  high voice singing over a stripped bed — the ordered Qveen
  outro, in the ordered seat, at the ordered quiet.
  **Confidence MEDIUM** — the strongest attribution this bench can
  make on this mix.

Net (INTERPRETATION): the performance's *architecture* — intro
room, two-part body, register dip, quiet sung tail — rhymes with
the ordered formation at its two ends and blurs in its middle.
The verse-by-verse trading pattern the brief ordered is **not
readable from this bench on this mix**, and the desk does not
pretend otherwise.

## 4. The Brightmoor Signature audit — the decisive variable (MEASURED, with a declared fallback)

The order: a four-note grand-piano motif that opens the track
**alone**, answers the end of **every hook**, and is the **last
sound before the hard stop** — identical wherever it plays.

**The primary instrument collapsed, and its collapse is data.**
The alone-window test (first sustained full-band arrival vs first
gated voice) reads: full-band arrival **0.20 s**, first gated
voice **39.26 s** (a gate artefact — §10), alone window **0.20 s**,
holding exactly **one** bed note: **F2** (onset 0.197 s, 0.511 s,
87.7 Hz). Either the render opens hot — full programme from the
first fifth of a second — or the solo figure itself plays above
the arrival threshold. On the primary instrument the motif seat is

This is a **bass figure**, not a melodic motif: F2–G#2–C3, the
F-minor triad turning in the low register, with the tracker

**The search.** Every run of four consecutive bed notes in the
file was tested exact (same four MIDI numbers) and transposed
(same intervals, any root). Result: the candidate sequence occurs
**only at its own origin** (0.197 s) — zero exact recurrences,
zero transposed recurrences, at the hook-answer seats, at every
measured segment end, and in the final seat (146.25–154.75 s).
The last pitched content before the fade is segment 12's sung
line, not a piano figure.

**Verdict: ABSENT at bench strength** — with the extraction caveat
carried in the open: the bench searched for a motif it could only
extract as an artefact, so what is proven is narrower than the
words. What stands proven: (a) no stable four-note figure recurs
at any ordered seat; (b) the track does not audibly open, to this
bench, with an alone piano motif — it opens with a low bass
figure under a rising room; (c) the final sound is a voice, not
the signature. If a four-note piano figure is in there, it lives
below the bed track's ability to hold it (dense, low, short), and
raising this tier needs a listening pass or a stem, not a bigger
autocorrelation. **No candidate canonical notation is printed** —
the condition for printing one (an identical recurrence) failed,
and the extracted figure is not clean enough to canonise. The
four-note signature, as a *measurable* fact of this render, did
not survive contact with the engine — the same law the Sigma
probes found on the earlier strikes, now for the motif.

## 5. Voices — two performers ordered; the register instrument confesses (MEASURED)

The brief ordered two distinct voice signatures: **his** — medium
pitch, NOT a deep bass, the testimony register; **hers** — Eve/Brat
rap band, hooks sung in a soprano. The bench weighed both on two
pitch tracks (bench 65–500 Hz; register pass 65–950 Hz), gated
voiced frames only, with class thresholds stated in the bench
before looking.

**The confound, measured.** Across the loud body, segment register
medians cluster at **350.0 / 355.6 / 352.8 / 364.5 Hz** again and
again — the tracker pinned to a cycling pitched tone at ~F4. That
is the piano-arpeggio bed itself passing the vocal gate (centred,
speech-band-rich) and out-voting the voices frame by frame. The
classes in §2 therefore describe, for most body segments, **the
bed's register — not a singer's**. Any document that read them as
voices would be lying with numbers; this one does not.

**The two-voice test fails by its own stated rule.** Pooling the
class split: her pool (13 segments) centres **364.5 Hz**; his pool
(1 segment) centres **131.6 Hz** — a separation of **17.64
semitones**, far past the 5-semitone bar — **but** the IQR spans
overlap (hers 187.5–551.2 Hz, his 104.0–273.9 Hz), and the "his"
pool is segment 0, the *instrumental intro*, whose 131.6 Hz centre
is the bass figure, not a rapper. The test's verdict: **does not
stand**. The bench cannot certify two distinct vocal performers
from pitch evidence on this mix.

What can be said, tiered:

- **His medium-pitch order: NOT CERTIFIABLE from this bench.**
  No body segment centres in the 100–160 Hz male-rap band; the
  gate rarely accepts the dense body's frames as voice at all
  (voiced densities 0.09–0.31), and where it does, it hears the
  bed. The absence of a deep-bass segment is *consistent* with
  the order — nothing in the file centres at a bass voice's
  65–100 Hz either — but consistency is not certification.
  **Confidence LOW.**
- **Her soprano order: consistent in range, inseparable from the
  bed.** The sung-high pool's medians (258–464 Hz) sit in a high
  female singing range, and the share of gated register frames
  ≥ 300 Hz reaches 0.84–0.95 in segments 2, 3, 5, 10, 11 — but the
  same numbers are what a piano figure at F4 would print. The one
  place the evidence sharpens is segment 12 (the outro): voiced
  density 0.81, stability collapsed to 0.46, centre 375.3 Hz —
  there, a lone high voice is the best explanation of the
  measurements. **Confidence LOW in the body, MEDIUM at the
  outro.**
- The founder's ear has already ruled on this strike ("Its nice"
  — a pass). The desk's pitch bench neither confirms nor
  contradicts him; it reports that on a mix this dense, with a
  pitched arpeggio bed cycling at F4, autocorrelation register
  evidence is **the wrong instrument for the duet question**, and
  says what would serve instead in §12.

## 9. The piano/violin check — no bowed-string signature found; confidence LOW (MEASURED)

The brief said **NO VIOLIN** — grand piano leads the strings. Two
physical tests were run:

- **Attack.** In the alone window the bench could weigh exactly
  one note: the opening F2, attack **28.0 ms** (10%→90% rise) —
  under the 30 ms struck-string line, i.e. struck-like, not
  bowed. A sample of one is a whisper, not a witness.
- **Vibrato.** The bench searched for sustained (≥ 0.35 s)
  instrumental pitched runs to weigh for 4–7 Hz bow wobble and
  found **zero**: the bed never sits still long enough — it
  arpeggiates. There is no sustained string tone in this mix for
  a violin to hide in, which is itself consistent with the
  ordered piano/arpeggio arrangement.

Reading: **UNREADABLE at full strength; no violin signature
found.** The one attack that could be weighed is piano-shaped,
and the vibrato instrument found nothing sustained to accuse.
Confidence **LOW** — absence of evidence, stated as absence, and
a synth string patch could imitate either signature besides.

## 10. Method notes and failures found on the way (marked, per house law)

1. **The alone window collapsed** (0.20 s): the RMS arrival
   threshold (−28 dBFS sustained) is crossed almost at once. Two
   suspects stand: the render opens hot, or a solo piano figure
   at performance level trips a threshold tuned for quiet intros.
   The fallback instrument was declared in the bench and marked
   wherever used; the primary result is printed, not buried.
2. **The vocal gate is bed-confounded on this mix.** A centred,
   speech-band-rich piano arpeggio passes the gate (band share
   ≥ 0.45, mid > side) and its periodicity out-votes rap
   intonation. Both prior intakes ran sparser beds; the gate's
   limits were found here, on the duet, and are recorded so the
   next duet intake brings a separation instrument instead.
3. **t_voice = 39.26 s is a gate artefact**, not a vocal entry:
   it is merely the first ≥ 0.3 s run the confounded gate would
   certify. No prose in this document treats it as an entry.
4. **Segment 13's features are void**: at −79.5 dB mean the tail
   is fade residue; its register median (348.6 Hz) is the tracker
   reading the bed's ghost. It is tabled for completeness and
   used for nothing.
5. **The melody sketch's 500 Hz cap** shadows a soprano's true
   line (her fundamentals ride partly above it); the sketch's
   high notes are the tracker's subharmonic shadows where she
   sings. The register pass (950 Hz) exists precisely because
   this failure was foreseen — and §5 records what it found.
