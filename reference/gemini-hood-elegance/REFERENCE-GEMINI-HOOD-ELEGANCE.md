## The measured off-spec items (coordinates, not verdicts)

1. **The Brightmoor Signature — ABSENT at bench strength.** The
   alone-window test collapsed (full-band arrival at 0.20 s; one
   bed note, F2, inside it). The declared fallback extracted the
   intro segment's opening figure — a low F-minor bass figure
   (F2–G#2–C3) with tracker octave-hops, not a clean four-note
   motif — and the whole-file search found that figure **only at
   its own origin**: zero exact and zero transposed recurrences
   at the hook-answer seats, at every segment end, and in the
   final seat. The track's last pitched content is the outro's
   voice, not a piano signature. No canonical notation is
   printed: the identical-recurrence condition failed and the
   extracted figure is not clean enough to canonise. Caveat
   carried in the open (FORM-MAP §4): a motif below the bed
   track's grip — dense, low, short — would read exactly this
   way; the tier rises with a listening pass or a stem, not with
   more autocorrelation.
2. **Key**: performed F (contested mode, minor-leaning) against
   the ordered G# minor. Third strike, third self-chosen key.
3. **Hat clock**: ≈246 BPM measured (the performance's own
   double) against the ordered 222 (hat-band ac 0.106 vs 0.232).
4. **Voices — not certifiable on this bench.** The register
   instrument is confounded by the pitched bed: segment medians
   pin to the arpeggio's cycling ~F4 tone, the two-voice test
   fails its own stated rule (IQR overlap; the "his" pool is the
   instrumental intro), and neither his medium-pitch order nor
   her soprano can be certified from pitch evidence here. The
   outro segment (voiced density 0.81, centre 375.3 Hz) is the
   one place a lone high voice is the best explanation of the
   numbers. This is an instrument finding, not a performance
   finding — the founder's ear has passed the strike.
5. **Violin check**: no bowed-string signature found — the one
   weighable attack (the opening F2) measures 28.0 ms,
   struck-like; the vibrato instrument found zero sustained
   instrumental runs to weigh (the bed arpeggiates; nothing
   sustains). Confidence LOW; absence of evidence stated as
   absence (FORM-MAP §9).
6. **The hard stop is a fade**: programme energy dies across
   ≈153.6–155.3 s to digital quiet; the ordered stop reads as a
   fast fade at the file's edge.
7. **Sigma**: not ordered, not probed, not claimed — recorded
   here so the silence in the record is a decision, not a gap.

## What the house takes from it

Three strikes in, the adopted road's law has a third clause. The
engine performs, counts when briefed tightly (Sovereign) — and
describes-but-does-not-do has now been measured on: Sigma (both
probes, two strikes), the in-performance arpeggio (Sovereign),
the ordered key (all three strikes), the ordered hat number
(here), and now the **identity element itself** — a signature
motif whose whole nature is recurrence — did not recur. What the
engine *did* deliver here is not nothing: the widest stereo of
the three strikes, a clean master with nothing for the ceiling
to confess, an architecture that rhymes with the ordered
formation at both ends, and — at the outro seat — the file's one
unambiguous voice: a lone high singer over a stripped bed,
exactly where the brief put her. If the Brightmoor Signature is
to exist as a fact and not a hope, the record now says how: it
must be struck by a hand that can repeat itself — fixed as
notation first, then placed — the same conclusion the desk
offered, unruled, when the brief was filed. The founder's ear
verdict stands: **"Its nice."** The measurements keep their
seats beside it.
