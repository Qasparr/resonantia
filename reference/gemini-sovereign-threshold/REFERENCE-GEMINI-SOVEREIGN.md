# REFERENCE — "SOVEREIGN AT THE THRESHOLD" (Gemini/Lyria, founder-struck)

## Provenance

On 2026-11-10 the founder struck this render in Gemini himself,
from the desk's filed brief (`studio-runs/gemini-49-bars/prompt.txt`,
pushed **faf3a3b**): a counted form — exactly 49 bars: intro 3 ·
Verse I 20 (bars 4–23) · refrain 3 (24–26) · Verse II 20 (27–46) ·
outro 3 (47–49) — over Detroit bounce in F minor, violin/piano/
harmonica front line, a solfeggio arpeggio ordered *inside* the
performance, a binaural Sigma pair ordered to *sweep* 13–31 Hz in
the pocket, one lead rap vocal. The brief wrote the first strike's
three measured omissions into the order; this intake weighs what
came back, item by item. The file of record is his upload:
`sovereign_at_the_threshold__1__1_ygcl.mp3`, 2,875,625 bytes,
SHA-256 `dc58584c7811…`, MP3, 44.1 kHz stereo, ≈192 kbps.
Phase-1 transcription in `FORM-MAP.md`; machine outputs in
`analysis-stats.json`, `grid-b-audit.json`, `chords.csv`,
`chords-grid-b.csv`, `melody-sketch.json`; the master and its
confessions in `manifest.json`.

## The house master (manifest.json carries the full report)

- Law applied by `pipeline.master_signal`: loudness first, then
  the ceiling — **and this time the ceiling won a round.** Gain
  −0.90 dB toward target; soft limiter; then a true-peak trim of
  **−0.16 dB**, because the struck render's overs (+0.79 dBTP)
  survived the gain. Result, verified from the struck file:
  **−14.17 LUFS** (0.17 under the −14 target), **true peak
  −1.02 dBTP**, 48 kHz, 119.562 s; the manifest confesses
  **`ceiling_overrode_loudness: true`** in the open — the exact
  confession the first strike's master never had to make (its
  trim was 0.0 and the flag false). The law is the same; the
  files are different; the record shows both.
- **Width: LEFT AS STRUCK** (corr 0.9352 in, 0.9352 out). No
  stems exist; the vocal bleeds into what side signal there is,
  and no conservative correction on this desk can be shown to
  leave the vocal untouched. In doubt, leave it — confessed.

  silence and the whisper are likewise left exactly as struck —
  an intake master applies the loudness/ceiling law, not an

## The measured off-spec items (coordinates, not verdicts)

1. **The counted form, on grid A:** intro exact; refrain seat
   missed by 4.00 bars; Verse II seat by 7.00; 45 grid bars
   against 49 ordered. **On grid B:** all seats within 2.00
   bars and the total span within ≈0.6 s — the brief's
   arithmetic nearly closes on the faster clock, and the grid
   itself is the contested quantity (FORM-MAP §1).
2. **Dead air inside the file:** exact digital zeros from
   ≈99.0 to ≈109.9 s — 10.9 s of nothing, mid-artifact — where
   neither grid's form has a silence.
3. **The outro's displacement:** the ordered outro (bars 47–49,
   a fading vamp on the refrain line) does not occur at its
   seat on either grid. What occurs instead is a whisper
   reprise at ≈110–118.5 s, after the silence, at −36…−63 dB,
   rhyming in chroma with the fall (+0.662) and reading the
   D-minor cadence at HIGH margins — an outro's ghost, eleven
   seconds and some fifty decibels away from its orders.
4. **The refrain declares no joint:** no 3-bar segment at
   bars 24–26 on either grid; on A the seat lies inside the
   loudest span; on B the 50.18 s seam crosses it. (The seam at
   50.70 s may be its exit — INTERPRETATION, LOW.)
5. **Key centre:** D minor measured (K-S 0.867) against F minor
   ordered. F stands third in the chroma and in the cadence —
   present, not crowned.
6. **Hot as delivered:** true peak +0.79 dBTP on a decode that
   itself peaks at +0.5 dBFS — no headroom, and overs beyond
   the first strike's. The master repairs it at the price the
   ceiling exacted (−14.17 LUFS, flag flown).
7. **Sigma:** not certifiable at the fixed seats, and the
   ordered *sweep* does not stand in the trajectory — median
   interaural difference 0.0 Hz against an ordered 13–31 Hz
   travel. The lines are the programme's, in both ears alike.
8. **Arpeggio:** not established — chord-step share 0.099
   against the 0.45 threshold, under a bed census headed by a
   marked tracker artefact. The brief's most specific musical
   order is the one the evidence can neither confirm nor
   cleanly deny (§ profile, and FORM-MAP §6.3/§8).

## What the house takes from it

1. **Counted form travels — imperfectly, but measurably.** The
   sheet (strike one) got a 53-bar song with an invented hook;
   the counted brief got a programme whose total span matches
   its 49 bars to ≈0.6 s on the clock that fits, with every
   interior seat within 2 bars on that clock. Plan-first
   generation counts when it is told to count; it counts in
   its own tempo and rounds where it pleases.
2. **The failure species changed.** Strike one's failures were
   of omission (tempo, headroom, width, Sigma). Strike two
   adds failures of *structure*: dead air inside the artifact
   and an outro displaced past a silence into a whisper. Only
   the grid-free instruments (the per-second contour, the
   exact-zero test) see them plainly — which is why this intake
   runs them as first-class benches, not afterthoughts.
3. **Quiet evidence is still evidence.** The file's most
   legible harmony is its quietest eight seconds: at −40 dB
   the template bench reads margins it never reaches at full
   level. The whisper, whatever the model meant by it, is the
   intake's best witness to the song's centre.
4. **The division of labour holds.** Gemini supplied what it
   supplies — a performed song, this time on a counted brief —
   and left the same four crafts to the desk: the clock's
   adjudication, the headroom, the record, and the Sigma
   question, which remains the founder's open call, unanswered
   by either strike.
